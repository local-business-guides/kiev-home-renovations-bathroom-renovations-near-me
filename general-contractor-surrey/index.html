Project scope, timing, and useful terms

A renovation plan should spell out the work being considered before the project moves forward. For a general contractor, it is especially helpful to talk through how separate tasks relate and what decisions are needed from you.

Key facts for planning:
- What it covers: The responsibilities depend on the project and the written agreement. Discuss which parts of the work are included and how coordination will be handled.
- Typical timing: Projects move through planning, selections, and construction, but there is no universal duration. Scope, material availability, site conditions, and any approvals that may apply can affect the schedule.
- What affects cost: Project size, the number and type of tasks, material choices, changes to the design, and unexpected repairs can all influence an estimate. Request a clear description of assumptions and exclusions.
- How to compare providers: Compare the proposed scope, schedule, estimate details, communication approach, and the process for approving changes. Make sure you understand who is responsible for each part of the work.

You may hear a few terms during planning. Scope of work is the description of the tasks included. Estimate is a projected cost based on the known details. Allowance is a budget amount set aside for a selection that has not yet been finalized. Change order documents an agreed change to the original plan. Ask for these terms to be explained in the context of your project.
