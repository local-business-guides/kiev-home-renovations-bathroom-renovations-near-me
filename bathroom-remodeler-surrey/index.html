Scope, schedule, and terms to know

The exact work varies by project. As a starting point, discuss which parts of the bathroom you want changed, what should stay, and whether you have priorities around layout, fixtures, finishes, or technology.

Key facts to keep in mind:
- What it covers: The agreed bathroom renovation work, based on the room and your goals. Confirm the included tasks and materials with the provider.
- Typical timing: There is no single schedule that fits every remodel. Planning and selections come before construction, and the overall time depends on the scope, material availability, and any conditions uncovered during the work.
- What affects cost: Room size, the amount of work, material and fixture choices, layout changes, and any repairs that become necessary can all affect the estimate. Ask for project details to be listed clearly, and confirm how changes are handled.
- How to compare providers: Review each proposed scope, schedule assumptions, communication plan, and estimate. Ask who your contact will be and what decisions need to be made before work starts.

A few common terms help during planning. Scope means the agreed list of work. Estimate is the projected cost based on the information available. Change order is a documented change to the planned work, which may affect cost or timing. Ask the remodeler to explain any term that is unclear.
